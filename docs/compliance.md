# Compliance

## Text messages

**Status on 2026-10-08:** Quo account open on a new number. Carrier registration not submitted. (423) 380-9120 not ported.

**Plan:** submit carrier registration once, for every message type, as soon as the new booking form with its consent checkboxes is public (Phase 3). Port the number only after approval. Setmore keeps sending its own reminders until launch.

Why wait: the only form collecting phone numbers today is Setmore's booking page, which cannot carry compliant consent wording.

### What registration needs

- Legal name exactly as on the EIN letter, including "LLC", and the address the IRS has on file.
- A live website with business details and contact information.
- Consent wording next to the submit button of every form that collects a phone number, linking to the privacy policy.
- Point of contact: the owner's personal cell number, not a VoIP number, and an email at the business domain if possible.
- Every message type to be sent: appointment, customer care, payment, review requests, follow-ups and offers.

Quo's guide says most approvals take 24 to 48 hours and some over 20 days. Fees: $19.50 once, $1.50 a month, $15 to resubmit after a rejection.

### Rules the system must follow

- Two separate consent checkboxes on the booking form, both unchecked by default, neither required:
  1. Appointment texts: confirmations, reminders, arrival updates, payment links and receipts.
  2. Follow-up texts: review requests, service reminders and offers.
- Record each consent with its date, source (web form or phone) and wording version.
- Phone bookings: ask for consent verbally and record it.
- Send follow-up texts only to customers with follow-up consent.
- Honor STOP, HELP and START automatically, with the reply wording from the compliance drafts.
- Every message starts with "Dixie Detail:" and carries opt-out wording where required.
