# 0019: Flag a second person at 8 one-person hours

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-07 · **Area:** crew

## Context

Long jobs happen and are allowed, but one person cannot always finish them in a day.

## Decision

Flag, never block. Show "full day" to the customer instead of hours.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded; the owner set this directly.

## Consequences

The owner decides whether to bring a helper.
