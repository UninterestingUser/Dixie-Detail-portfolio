# 0020: Arrival at 8:00, 8:30 or 9:00 AM, closed Sundays, two days' notice

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-07 · **Area:** booking

## Context

The owner's working pattern.

## Decision

The same three arrival times for every service. No same-day or next-day booking. Holidays and days off close days.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded; the owner set this directly.

## Consequences

Availability is simple to compute and explain.
