# 0037: Approved out-of-area addresses, with no Tennessee tax

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-11 · **Area:** tax

## Context

One regular customer lives just over the state line in Cohutta, Georgia, where the work is performed.

## Decision

An approved address passes the service-area check and gets normal drive times. Georgia's rate is a per-state setting at 0%, based on Georgia letter ruling LR SUT-2019-11.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded; the owner set this directly.

## Consequences

The owner is to confirm with the accountant, including any Georgia income tax filing.
