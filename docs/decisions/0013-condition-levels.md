# 0013: Three condition levels add 0%, 10% or 20%

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-07 · **Area:** pricing

## Context

Dirtier vehicles take longer. The Armstrong booking template the owner chose uses condition levels.

## Decision

Three levels to start, with the owner's own descriptions, applied to the base price of the three main services only.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded; the owner set this directly.

## Consequences

Add-ons, paint correction, headlights and maintenance plans are not affected.
