# 0031: Website design direction: red base, navy accent, familiar sections kept

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-10 · **Area:** website

## Context

The owner reviewed mockups of the homepage and booking flow and gave direction in two rounds.

## Decision

Red #CC0000 for buttons, the band and review cards; navy #002D65 for labels, prices, links and the estimate card. Hero with 40% shade, plain red band, shine section, reviews over a locked background with 50% shade after "We come to you", footer social icons.

## Alternatives considered

- None recorded; the owner set this directly.

## Consequences

Mockups live on the design canvas "Dixie Detail Website Mockups". The booking flow shows only reached steps and reveals the next card on completion.
