# Customer booking flow

This is how the website's booking page at `/book/` works, built in Phase 3, Step 6 to the mockups approved on the design canvas "Dixie Detail Website Mockups" ([decision 0105](../decisions/0105-the-website-on-cloudflare-pages.md)). It runs on the staging site until the Phase 5 launch; until then customers book through Setmore. Each endpoint it calls is described in [public-api.md](public-api.md). The code is in `site/src/pages/book.astro` and `site/src/scripts/booking/`.

The flow follows the Armstrong "Build Your Detail" screens the owner chose as a model, with a running estimate card, and goes one step further: it ends in a confirmed appointment instead of an estimate. Armstrong's wax-upgrade step is left out because ceramic wax is included in every detail.

The page opens under "Get your price and book" with "Complete the form and see your estimate immediately. Nothing is charged until work is completed." (owner, 2026-11-19).

## The seven steps

1. **Vehicle.** Year, then make, then model. The homepage's "See your exact price" picker opens this page with them filled in. Vehicle classes are internal: nothing on the page names the class (owner, 2026-11-18). A cab or third-row question appears only when the vehicle data cannot settle the size variant. Once answered it stays shown, with the answer selected, and can be changed; the prices follow the answer. Motorcycle models appear in the same year, make and model lists as cars, each with a small "Motorcycle" label, so a Honda Gold Wing and a Honda Accord are easy to tell apart; a motorcycle is in the Motorcycles class, with no size question ([decision 0072](../decisions/0072-motorcycles-class.md)). A vehicle not in the lists, such as a pre-1984 classic, is chosen from eight photo cards, one per vehicle class, Motorcycles among them; the Full-size Vans card, "Ask for a quote", leads to a quote request instead (below), because that class is not booked online (decisions [0083](../decisions/0083-applying-the-size-based-classes.md) and [0099](../decisions/0099-quote-requests-with-photos.md)). The class, size and question come from one lookup on the server ([vehicle-classification.md](vehicle-classification.md#the-lookup)). A "My vehicle isn't listed" link opens the photo cards too, where the customer can type the year, make and model they know and pick the size when the class has more than one. The Color box (two thirds of the row) and the "My vehicle isn't listed" link (a third) share the last row; the color is optional and used only to tell a returning customer's identical vehicles apart and never changes the price ([decision 0067](../decisions/0067-color-tells-identical-vehicles-apart.md)). For a vehicle booked by quote there is no separate button: under the Color box the page says, in bold, "Full-size vans vary in size and condition." and on the next line "Request a quote and we'll reply with your estimate, usually within a day.", and the step's button reads **Request quote** and opens the quote request.
2. **Service and add-ons ("Choose your service").** Service cards, in the order Full Detail, Interior Only, Exterior Only, 1-Step Paint Correction and 2-Step Paint Correction, show the prices for the vehicle's class and size, before any condition adjustment, each with the owner's short text from [data/site-content.yaml](../../data/site-content.yaml). Picking one asks the server for a quote, and the estimate card fills in. Add-ons, under "Available add-ons", are tick boxes with their added price, such as "+ $95", on one line even on a phone, in the order Carpet Extraction, Seat Extraction, Headlight Restoration, shown once a service is picked and only those that go with it ([decision 0106](../decisions/0106-add-ons-go-with-listed-services.md)): Carpet and Seat Extraction with Full Detail or Interior Only, and Headlight Restoration with Full Detail, Exterior Only or a paint correction. Changing the service drops a ticked add-on that doesn't go with the new one. Headlight Restoration is never a service card of its own. Maintenance plans never appear here. A motorcycle is offered only Motorcycle Detail, with no add-ons.
3. **Condition ("What is the condition of the interior?").** Only when the chosen service covers the interior: Full Detail or Interior Only ([decision 0103](../decisions/0103-service-levels-for-interior-work-and-level-3-by-quote.md)). Every other service, Exterior Only and Motorcycle Detail included, skips this step and has no condition level. Three cards with plain descriptions, the first picked to start: "Normal wear and tear" (Level 1) adds nothing, "Needs extra attention" (Level 2) adds 10%, shown on the estimate card as "Needs Attention (+10%)", and "Let's talk" (Level 3) has no price or time. With Level 3 picked the step shows only "Unique situations require manual quoting. Hit the button below to tell us what you're dealing with and add photos if you can. We'll respond with a quote, usually within 24 hours.", and its one button reads **Request quote** instead of "Continue to location"; it opens a quote request carrying the service, the level and the add-ons already ticked (below).
4. **Location.** The service address, validated as the customer types (see below). It is checked against the service area and sets the sales tax rate, with a reminder that water and power are needed. An address in the area shows only "In our service area", in bold on light green; the estimate card shows the tax. An address outside the area shows only the server's "Sorry, that address is outside the area we serve…" message, with the business's number, on light red.
5. **Date and time.** A month calendar, then the arrival times for the day picked. Only a day the availability check gives at least one start time for the chosen job can be picked. Every other day is grayed out and crossed out and can't be selected: a closed weekday, a holiday or other date exception, Time Off, a fully booked day, a day the job can't fit, a day too soon for the minimum notice and a day past the booking window (owner, 2026-11-19). The server lists every day in the window, a closed one with no times ([public-api.md](public-api.md#open-times-get-open-times)). The start times come from that weekday's start-times setting (8:00, 8:30 and 9:00 AM on every open day at launch), and a start time is shown only when the whole time on site fits. See [scheduling-and-calendars.md](scheduling-and-calendars.md).
6. **Details.** First name, a last name (optional, though its label doesn't say so), mobile number and preferred payment method, under "Nothing is charged now. Payment is due when the work is finished." The payment methods are one list, one entry per method, in the order of `payment_methods_accepted` in [data/business.yaml](../../data/business.yaml): Cash, Card, Venmo, PayPal, Cash App and Bitcoin. Cash App is one choice; its two routes, and the owner-only check and trade, are never shown ([decision 0110](../decisions/0110-one-website-list-of-payment-methods.md)).
7. **Review and confirm.** The full estimate. The vehicle line ends with the color in parentheses, such as "2025 Chevrolet Silverado · extended or crew cab (red)". The size detail ("extended or crew cab", "three rows") shows only when the customer answered a question for it; a vehicle the data settled, such as a 2015 Honda Accord (both body styles have two seat rows and price the same), shows just its name (owner, 2026-11-18). Then the services are joined with " + ", and the condition uses the card's wording. Next to the Book button is one unchecked consent checkbox, not required, for texts about the appointment, a review request after the service included ([decision 0108](../decisions/0108-one-text-consent.md)), in the exact wording the server serves ([data/consent-wording.yaml](../../data/consent-wording.yaml)), with the Privacy Policy and Text Message Terms linked. A Cloudflare Turnstile check runs when the customer presses Book, out of sight unless Cloudflare asks them to tick it. Then the confirmation, with the booking reference and an "Add to my calendar" file, worded for how the booking is confirmed (below). The booking refers to the server's quote, never to a price sent from the browser. A quote lasts 2 hours (a setting); booking an expired one, or one the chosen day would price differently, shows a fresh quote to confirm instead of booking at a new price. A time someone else took a moment earlier is refused, and the form offers the open times again.

## Confirming a new customer's booking

A booking holds its time as soon as it is made, but only a known customer's is confirmed at once ([decision 0112](../decisions/0112-confirming-bookings-from-new-customers.md)). A known customer is a number that quietly matches a customer with at least one completed job ([returning-customers.md](returning-customers.md#known-customers-and-confirming)). Every other booking is a real appointment that waits for the customer to confirm it. The server's answer says which, and the confirmation screen reads:

| Who | What they see |
| --- | --- |
| A new number, consent box ticked | "Check your phone. We've texted you to confirm your appointment. Reply YES to confirm it." |
| A new number, box not ticked | "We'll call you to confirm your appointment." |
| A known customer | The confirmation as before: "We'll text you a confirmation." when the box was ticked |

Each is followed by the booking reference. With the box ticked, the confirmation request text is queued for Phase 4's sender ([text-messages.md](text-messages.md#the-confirmation-request)). Until Phase 4 sends texts the first message is shown anyway: the site isn't public before then. Without the box ticked, the job goes on the control app's "needs you" list as "Call to confirm". In the control app an unconfirmed job is yellow, and so is its Jobs calendar event ([control-app.md](control-app.md#unconfirmed-jobs)).

**The caps.** A new number can hold one open unconfirmed booking, and five new customers' bookings can be unconfirmed at once (scheduling settings). A booking past either cap, or one for a number that already has a job that day, is not booked: the server keeps it as a request with the quote the customer saw, and the form shows "Request received" with "Thanks! We've got your request for this time. We'll confirm it by text or call. Your request number is …", and no calendar file. Every such booking gets the same words, whatever the reason, so the page never says whether a number already has a booking ([decision 0113](../decisions/0113-one-answer-for-a-booking-that-becomes-a-request.md)). The request goes on "needs you" as a booking request, and the owner approves it, which books that quote, or declines it.

## How the steps behave

- The step indicator at the top is display only. It shows finished steps, the current step and grayed-out future steps; customers cannot jump ahead by clicking it.
- Only steps already reached are shown. Finishing a step reveals the next card below and scrolls to it. Earlier cards stay visible and can still be changed.
- "Start over" is a separate outlined button below the estimate card, not a text link. On a phone the bottom bar carries a Start over button in place of a Continue button. It asks first, "Start over? Your answers will be cleared.", with **Keep my answers** and **Start over**, so a stray tap doesn't wipe the form (owner, 2026-11-19). The continue buttons in each step stay.
- Changing an earlier step makes it the current step again: the steps after it hide until it is finished, keeping what they held. A new choice that changes the price asks the server for a new quote, and a new quote clears the time picked, because its time on site may differ.
- The Continue button says what is still missing, such as "Pick a service to continue", until the step is complete.

## The estimate card

- **Always in view.** It sits beside the steps on a desktop, and becomes a navy bar pinned to the bottom of a phone screen, with the Start over button, that opens into a full sheet when tapped.
- **Builds line by line.** The vehicle, the service, each add-on, the condition adjustment once the condition step is reached, sales tax once the address is known, the total and the estimated time on site (setup, service time and breakdown together). Level 2 lengthens the time as well as the price, so changing the level changes both ([decision 0065](../decisions/0065-condition-lengthens-service-time.md)). Level 3 has no price or time, so with it picked the card shows "Ask for a quote" in place of the total and time ([decision 0103](../decisions/0103-service-levels-for-interior-work-and-level-3-by-quote.md)). Before the address, the card says the total is before sales tax.
- **Shows the server's numbers only.** Every price, total and time on the card comes from the server's quote, shown as it arrives; the website does no arithmetic of its own ([decision 0059](../decisions/0059-quoted-price-is-price-of-record.md)). A check in CI refuses any arithmetic beside a money name in the booking code, and the browser tests use a fake server whose totals are deliberately not the sums of their lines.
- **Shows "full day"** instead of hours when the one-person time on site is 8 hours or more, condition time included.
- **Sets expectations.** It states that the final price is confirmed when the vehicle is seen, and that nothing is charged until the job is done.

## Address entry is validated as the customer types

The address field works like a Shopify checkout: suggestions appear as the customer types, and the address must be a real one. The system uses Google Places Autocomplete (New), restricted to US addresses and biased to the service area, with a session token. Picking a suggestion sends the server only the place ID and the session token. The server looks the place up itself with its own key, asking only for the address parts, formatted address and location; that call ends the session, so the suggestions before it are not billed. The server then runs the service-area check and the tax lookup ([sales-tax.md](sales-tax.md)). Coordinates from the browser are never used. There is no separate "check" button. Addresses outside the service area are refused unless the owner has approved them. The Google Address Validation API may be added later to catch missing unit numbers. The field calls Google's Places Autocomplete (New) web service from the browser with the browser key (`PUBLIC_GOOGLE_MAPS_BROWSER_KEY`), so the session token is the website's own and the server's lookup can end the same session. Suggestions can be picked with the arrow keys and Enter. When Google refuses or fails, such as once the day's limit is used up, the form switches to the request a time form below, with the address typed.

**Lookup sessions** ([decision 0115](../decisions/0115-address-lookup-sessions-and-a-public-share.md)). When the customer reaches the location step, the page opens an address lookup session with a Turnstile token, out of sight. A session allows about 10 lookups in 30 minutes; when one runs out the page opens another and tries again, without the customer noticing. Once the session is open, the page gets a fresh Turnstile token ready for the Book button, since each token is used once. When the day's public share of the server's lookups is used up, picking an address says "We can't look up addresses online any more today. Type your address and send your booking as a request, and we'll confirm by text or call.", and the form becomes the request a time form with a typed address.

## Phone number entry

The mobile number field formats itself as (###) ###-#### while the customer types and requires 10 digits. The number is stored as 10 digits and used for quiet matching; see [returning-customers.md](returning-customers.md).

## The Color box

The vehicle step's Color box is optional free text, such as "silver" or "dark blue", and the step can be finished without it. It is used only when quiet matching finds a returning customer with more than one vehicle of the same make, model and year: the color picks the right one. A blank color, or one that matches none of them, never stops the booking; the appointment books and the control app asks the owner which vehicle it was. The color is not a price input, so it never appears on the estimate card or changes the quote. The full rule is in [returning-customers.md](returning-customers.md).

## Quote requests for jobs the site can't price

Two kinds of job can't be booked online:

- **A vehicle in a class booked by quote,** today only Full-size Vans ([decision 0099](../decisions/0099-quote-requests-with-photos.md)). The vehicle step's **Request quote** button opens the quote request, which starts "Full-size Vans vary in size and condition and require a manual estimate. Give us some details about the job and we'll reply with a price, usually within a day."
- **Full Detail or Interior Only at Service Level 3** ([decision 0103](../decisions/0103-service-levels-for-interior-work-and-level-3-by-quote.md)). The condition step's **Request quote** button opens the quote request, keeping the vehicle, the service and the add-ons already chosen. It starts "Unique situations require a manual estimate. Give us some details about the job and we'll reply with a price, usually within a day."

The quote request takes:

- the service wanted, or "Not sure" (a Level 3 request keeps its service), with the condition level and any add-ons. The list reads Not sure, Full Detail, Interior Only, Exterior Only, 1-Step Paint Correction, 2-Step Paint Correction (owner, 2026-11-19), whatever order the API sends. Only the services for the vehicle's class are offered: Motorcycle Detail only for a motorcycle, and never for a car, truck or van (`only_for` in [data/site-content.yaml](../../data/site-content.yaml));
- a required description of what's needed, under "Provide relevant details about the vehicle (size, interior configuration, etc.) and what you need done.";
- the service address, checked as in step 4;
- the name (the last name optional) and mobile number;
- an optional note on preferred days, "Such as weekdays, a specific date, etc.";
- the consent checkbox, just above "Send my request";
- up to 6 photos, optional, under "Photos are optional, but helpful in providing an accurate estimate. Add up to 6 photos." Each uploads as it is picked, so a slow one never loses the form, and shows a thumbnail, a remove button and its upload progress. The browser shrinks a photo to at most 2560 pixels before sending it when it can read it; iPhone HEIC photos it can't read go as they are, and are accepted.
- a Turnstile check when the first photo is picked, to open the upload session, and one when the request is sent. The card asks Turnstile for a token as soon as it opens and keeps one ready, getting a new one before it expires (tokens last five minutes), so the first photos upload at once. If Turnstile still fails after its own retries (below), the photos stay in the card, marked "Not added", with "We couldn't get the photos ready to send just now. Try them again, or send the request without them and text the photos to (423) 380-9120." and a "Try the photos again" button; nothing typed is lost.

Sending it books nothing and holds no time: the customer is told Dixie Detail will reply with a price. The owner sees the request with its photos in the control app, with the Level 2 price beside a Level 3 request for reference. A class or level the owner later sets to "Ask for a quote", such as RVs or boats, uses the same form.

## Other cases

- **A second vehicle the same day** is taken as a request. When quiet matching finds the customer already has a job that day, the booking becomes a booking request, with the same answer as a booking past the caps above; nothing says why ([public-api.md](public-api.md#booking-post-bookings), [returning-customers.md](returning-customers.md#a-second-vehicle-the-same-day)). The time is checked first, so at launch this is a second motorcycle; a day takes one job otherwise.


## When the server can't be reached

The site is static on Cloudflare, so its pages and built-in prices load even when the home server is down. When the booking page can't reach the server (no answer, a timeout or a server error), or Google's address suggestions fail, a notice says so and the form becomes a **request a time** form ([decision 0105](../decisions/0105-the-website-on-cloudflare-pages.md)):

- **The vehicle** is picked from the photo cards, with the year, make and model typed if known, and the size when the class has more than one. A vehicle the server already settled stays settled.
- **The service, add-ons and condition** work as above. The estimate card shows the server's own total for the job from the price table baked into the page when it was built (`GET price-table`), marked as before sales tax and from that pricebook version. The browser looks the total up and never works it out.
- **The address** uses Google's suggestions while they work; otherwise it is typed, and the owner checks it.
- **The date and time** are a preferred day, a preferred arrival from that weekday's start times or "any time", and an optional note. Nothing is booked.
- **Details and the consent** are as in steps 6 and 7, with Turnstile when the customer presses Send.

Cloudflare's offline piece takes the form. It checks the Turnstile token, tries the server at once, signed, and otherwise holds the request until the server answers, replaying it every 5 minutes ([public-api.md](public-api.md#offline-requests-post-time-requests), [decision 0100](../decisions/0100-public-api-quotes-and-bookings.md)). The server prices it on arrival, keeps it as a request rather than an appointment, and flags it for the owner if the price differs from what the customer saw. The customer is told Dixie Detail will confirm the day and price by text or call.

A quote request sent while the server can't be reached goes the same way, without photos: the form says photos can be sent later and gives the number to text them to.

Any network failure, timeout or server error (5xx) from the API counts as the server being unreachable, whenever it happens. That includes Cloudflare's own error pages, such as the 530 while the tunnel is down: they carry no CORS headers, so the browser sees them as a network failure. A call the server answers with a refusal (4xx) is not an outage; the form shows the server's message.

## When a send fails

No send waits forever. A booking, a quote request and a request a time each give up after a set time. A Turnstile check gets 15 seconds, or 45 once Cloudflare asks the customer to tick the box; one that times out, reports an error or returns no token is tried again on its own with a freshly drawn widget, up to three more times, 1.5 seconds apart, before anything is shown (owner, 2026-11-18; `site/src/scripts/booking/turnstile.js`). Then 15 seconds for the server, or 20 for Cloudflare's offline piece, which tries the server first. Each photo upload gives up after 3 minutes. A Turnstile check that still can't start, reports an error or returns no token after those tries counts as a failure, and nothing is sent. When a send fails, the button comes back, a plain message says nothing was sent and gives the business phone number, and everything the customer entered stays in the form, so trying again is one press. A booking that fails because the server can't be reached becomes the request a time form instead, keeping the vehicle, service and details.
