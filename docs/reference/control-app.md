# Control app

The control app is a private web app on the home server, opened in a browser on the Fedora workstations and installed to the Pixel's home screen. It is reached only over the owner's Tailscale network, at `https://server.example-tailnet.ts.net`, and is never exposed to the public internet. Phase 1 built its shell and the screens for customers, the schedule and jobs, staff and the pricebook, and Phase 2 the vehicle catalog's; later phases add the rest of the areas below. How to install it is in [../guides/installing-the-control-app.md](../guides/installing-the-control-app.md).

## What is built

- **The "needs you" list.** The home screen opens with only what needs a decision, newest first, with a count ([decision 0107](../decisions/0107-default-staff-and-needs-you-for-decisions.md)): "Call to confirm" for a new customer's booking without text consent, with call and text links and **Mark confirmed** ([Unconfirmed jobs](#unconfirmed-jobs)); quote requests; offline requests to price; booking requests (a website booking taken as a request, [decision 0113](../decisions/0113-one-answer-for-a-booking-that-becomes-a-request.md)); returning-customer differences (website bookings whose name, address or vehicle differs from the record); Time Off over a booked job; a job the calendar sync has failed to push three times in a row; and sales tax rates waiting for approval. A new booking is not an item, and neither is staffing, since every new booking gets the default staff. An appointment has at most one item: when several things need a decision on one job, the item names the others ("Also: …"). Each item opens the screen that handles it. Test records show a "Test" badge. Push notifications come in Phase 4 and will announce new bookings; until then the Jobs calendar event on the phone is the notice of a new booking. See [Website bookings and requests](#website-bookings-and-requests).
- **When the website's address lookups run out.** The Schedule screen says so when the day's public share of Place Details calls is used up: the website then takes typed addresses as requests until midnight Pacific time, while the control app keeps looking addresses up from the reserve ([decision 0115](../decisions/0115-address-lookup-sessions-and-a-public-share.md)).
- **The shell.** Every screen shares one layout, designed for the phone first. A bar along the bottom of the phone screen holds **Schedule**, **Customers**, **Staff**, **Pricebook**, **Catalog** and **Account**, six large targets within reach of a thumb; each form's **Save** and **Cancel** buttons, and **Add a customer**, sit just above it. The app opens on the schedule, in the view last used on that device. On a screen 900 pixels wide or more, the bar moves to the top. The colors are the brand colors in [data/business.yaml](../../data/business.yaml): navy for the top bar, red for the main buttons.
- **Installable.** A web app manifest, home-screen icons (white "DD" on the brand red, drawn by `tools/make_app_icons.py`) and a service worker let Chrome install the app. When the server cannot be reached, such as with Tailscale off, the installed app shows a "Can't reach the control app" page. The service worker keeps nothing else, so no customer data is stored on the device.
- **Sign-in.** Every page needs a signed-in user, except the sign-in page, the health page and the three files that installing needs. Sign-in works over the HTTPS tailnet address with secure cookies; how is in [decision 0068](../decisions/0068-control-app-https-static-and-sign-in.md). **Account** shows who is signed in, links superusers to the Django admin, and signs out. After 5 failed sign-ins for one username, or from one address, within 15 minutes, sign-ins for it are refused for 15 minutes with a plain message; a manager sees and clears lockouts on **Account, Sign-in lockouts** ([decision 0124](../decisions/0124-sign-in-lockout.md)).
- **Customers.** Every customer listed, those with the most completed jobs first, then the most recent visit, then last name, each with their count of completed jobs, and filtered by name or by phone number in any format as you type in the search box at the top (htmx), with **Add a customer** fixed at the bottom of the screen; a customer page with contact details, second contacts, addresses, vehicles, notes, every appointment with its lines, total and payments, and the canceled jobs below them with who canceled and the notice given; and adding and editing customers and vehicles. The steps are in [../guides/finding-a-customer.md](../guides/finding-a-customer.md).
- **Schedule and jobs.** Five views like the Google Calendar app, picked from a bar of icons that stays along the top of the Schedule screen (Google's Material Symbols, served by the app so they work offline; decision 0097) and remembered on each device in a cookie: Schedule, a list of the days with jobs that loads more as it scrolls either way; Day, 3 Days and Week, with hour rows, a column a day, each job a block from arrival to leaving, closed days shaded and a line at the current time; and Month, a grid with "+N more" for a full day. Weeks start on Sunday, and swiping moves on a phone (decision 0096). Booking a job for a customer and vehicle with the price built live by the pricing engine as the service, add-ons, condition, crew and date change, showing the service time and the time on site; the add-ons go only with the services the pricebook lists for them, and a manager can add another with a reason that is logged and shown on the job (decision 0106); the condition level shows only for Full Detail and Interior Only, with Level 1 picked, and Level 3 asks for the price and time on site by hand, kept as a quote record, with the Level 2 price shown for reference (decision 0103); maintenance plans as a single visit or a repeating Monthly or Quarterly series; changing a job, re-pricing it only with a reason; marking it booked or completed; canceling it from a popup as the customer or the business, or removing a job created by mistake for good (decision 0095); and the final price with an on-site adjustment and its reason. The scheduling rules are checked as the date, time or job changes, starting before the services are chosen with the rules that don't depend on them (decision 0097): an alert under the date and time names each rule broken, and a manager books off hours by giving a reason, which is logged and shown on the job (decisions 0094 and 0096). The steps are in [../guides/booking-a-job.md](../guides/booking-a-job.md), and the rules behind them in [decision 0069](../decisions/0069-booking-staffing-and-publishing-in-the-control-app.md).
- **The Google calendars.** Every booked job is mirrored to the Jobs calendar, with a Drive to job block, within seconds of each change, and the Time Off calendar is copied every 5 minutes and when a booking is sent (decision 0098). The home screen lists, under **Calendar**:
  - Time Off over a booked job, with **Keep**, **Reschedule** and **Cancel**
  - events added to the Jobs calendar by hand, which count as busy time until moved or deleted
  - Jobs events put back after an edit in Google, with **OK**.

  A job's page shows its own calendar notes. The home screen and the booking form warn when the Time Off copy is over an hour old, or when the calendars are not connected. The rules are in [scheduling-and-calendars.md](scheduling-and-calendars.md#calendar-sync-and-conflict-rules).
- **Website bookings and requests.** The review queue for quiet matching and the screens for each kind of website request; see [below](#website-bookings-and-requests) and [../guides/handling-website-bookings.md](../guides/handling-website-bookings.md).
- **Staff.** The staff page and the service page, with the assign-staff control on each job; see below and [../guides/assigning-staff.md](../guides/assigning-staff.md).
- **Pricebook.** Prices by service and vehicle class laid out like the price matrix, editing a draft, and publishing it with an effective date after deciding each affected booked job; see [pricebook-changes.md](pricebook-changes.md) and [../guides/editing-the-pricebook.md](../guides/editing-the-pricebook.md).
- **Vehicle catalog.** The **Catalog** tab: looking a vehicle up by year, make and model, with its class, size, price basis, each source's facts, how the class was reached, a test price, its overrides and its history; the classification rules in plain words, each change previewed before it applies; the vehicle class settings; the review queue, with one-tap and many-at-once decisions; the differences screen, where each customer's vehicle whose class differs from the catalog's is settled in one tap; and the approval list, where each year range of every model in the job history and the spot-check list is approved or changed. The schedule shows how many model years wait in the queue and, beside it, the last monthly vehicle data refresh: its date, whether it succeeded, and how many models it added and changed and model years it sent to the queue ([decision 0081](../decisions/0081-monthly-vehicle-data-refresh.md)). A customer's vehicle gets its own class on its edit screen. Only a manager changes anything. The steps are in [../operations/using-the-catalog-screens.md](../operations/using-the-catalog-screens.md), and the rules behind them in [decision 0076](../decisions/0076-catalog-screens-in-the-control-app.md), [decision 0077](../decisions/0077-linking-vehicles-to-the-catalog.md) and [decision 0079](../decisions/0079-approval-list.md).
- **Vehicles are named with their color** wherever they are listed or picked, as in "2019 Honda Accord, silver", so a customer's identical vehicles can be told apart ([decision 0067](../decisions/0067-color-tells-identical-vehicles-apart.md)).
- **The Django admin** at `/admin/` opens only for superusers, for fixing data by hand. It lists every business record, with its change history.

## Rules the control app follows

- **Linux-first by being a web app.** Nothing to install or update on any workstation. It can be wrapped in its own desktop window later without a rewrite.
- **Changes are traceable.** Every price or description change records who made it and when, and can be undone.
- **Server-rendered pages, phone first.** Screens are Django pages with htmx, designed for the phone first and installable to the home screen; the Django admin is for superusers only. See [decision 0046](../decisions/0046-control-app-django-htmx.md).
- **Everyone signs in.** Each person has their own account, and the sign-in page is reachable only over Tailscale. See [decision 0052](../decisions/0052-django-sign-in-over-tailscale.md).
- **GnuCash stays the books.** The app keeps the sales side and hands GnuCash balanced entries to import.

## Staff authorized per service

One authorization record links each staff member to each service they may perform ([decision 0061](../decisions/0061-staff-authorized-per-service.md)). It covers every active service, add-ons included; history-only services and custom lines are left out.

- **Two screens, one record.** The staff page shows a person's services as switches, and the service page shows a service's staff as switches. Both edit the same record, which saves at each tap and keeps who changed it, so there is nothing to keep in sync.
- **Managers** (staff with the owner role, so only the owner today) are authorized for every service without a record. A new service therefore starts with only managers authorized. Only a manager changes authorizations.

## Assigning staff to a job

- **The assign-staff control.** Each job's page lists every active staff member to assign. Qualified staff are listed first; anyone else appears grayed out, with the services they aren't authorized for. A booked job with no one assigned shows "No staff assigned" in every schedule view. Staffing is never a "needs you" item ([decision 0107](../decisions/0107-default-staff-and-needs-you-for-decisions.md)).
- **Two-person jobs.** The two people's authorizations together must cover every service on the job; neither needs all of them.
- **Training.** A manager can assign someone not yet authorized after a warning naming the services no one on the crew is authorized for; the reason is recorded on that person's assignment.
- **Crew size.** Assigning a different number of people than the job was booked with (the service's default unless changed) recalculates its service time and time on site (crew time and the setup and breakdown split), and warns if the job now overlaps another that day. The price does not change.
- **Default staff.** Every new booking, from the control app, the website or a website request, gets the default staff member, whoever else qualifies and whatever the crew size ([decision 0107](../decisions/0107-default-staff-and-needs-you-for-decisions.md)). The setting is on **Account → Scheduling settings**, under Staff, set to Kris Grassman at launch and seeded from `staff_assignment.default_staff` in [data/booking-rules.yaml](../../data/booking-rules.yaml). Change the crew on the job when someone else goes, and add the second person to a two-person job there. A quote request booked with a crew checked when it was priced gets those people instead.
- **Without a default staff member,** or when that person is inactive or not authorized for the job's services, a one-person job with exactly one qualified active staff member is assigned to them ([decision 0061](../decisions/0061-staff-authorized-per-service.md)). This is a setting, on by default, in [data/booking-rules.yaml](../../data/booking-rules.yaml).
- **Everyone assigned** gets the job and its time-to-leave alert.

## Unconfirmed jobs

A website booking from a new customer holds its time but waits to be confirmed ([decision 0112](../decisions/0112-confirming-bookings-from-new-customers.md), [booking-flow.md](booking-flow.md#confirming-a-new-customers-booking)). Every booking made in the control app, a website request booked here included, is confirmed, and so is a website booking from a known customer (a number with a completed job).

- **Yellow.** An unconfirmed job is yellow in every schedule view (Schedule, Day, 3 Days, Week and Month) and on its page, with its reason: "Waiting for a text reply" (the customer ticked the text consent box and was sent, or in Phase 4 will be sent, a confirmation request) or "Call to confirm" (they didn't). Its Jobs calendar event uses Google's yellow event color, Banana, and goes back to the calendar's own color once confirmed ([scheduling-and-calendars.md](scheduling-and-calendars.md)).
- **The banner.** The top of the Schedule screen lists the unconfirmed jobs that start within 48 hours (the scheduling setting "List unconfirmed jobs starting within, hours"), each with **Call** and **Text** links and **Mark confirmed**.
- **"Call to confirm"** goes on "needs you" as soon as a booking without text consent is made, with the same links and button.
- **Mark confirmed.** A manager can mark any unconfirmed job confirmed, from the banner, "needs you" or the job's page, with an optional note. The job records how it was confirmed (known customer, booked in the control app, text reply, or marked by a manager), when, and by whom, and the change goes in its history. A confirmed job's page says how.
- **The text link** appears only when appointment-text consent is on record, as on the request screens, so a "Call to confirm" job usually shows the call link alone.
- **The caps.** The scheduling settings screen's **Confirming new customers** section holds the two caps: unconfirmed bookings per new customer's number (1) and from new customers at once (5). Past either, the website takes a booking request instead of a booking.

## Website bookings and requests

Built in Phase 3, Step 5 ([decision 0101](../decisions/0101-website-bookings-in-the-control-app.md)); the steps are in [../guides/handling-website-bookings.md](../guides/handling-website-bookings.md).

- **The review queue.** **Account → Review queue** lists every website booking whose name, address or vehicle differs from the customer record it was quietly matched to, newest first ([returning-customers.md](returning-customers.md)). A job's page links to its own differences. Each difference shows the booked version beside the record's and offers the four actions: update the record (or, for identical vehicles, pick the one it was), keep the record, add alongside, or move the booking to a new customer. Every choice is logged on the difference with who, when and what it did. The job keeps the address and vehicle as booked whatever is chosen, so its price, tax and drive time never change.
- **Website requests.** **Account → Website requests** lists the three kinds of request that are not booked jobs ([public-api.md](public-api.md)), and each is in the "needs you" list while it waits. Each request screen has the customer's number with **Call** and **Text** links; the text link appears only when appointment-text consent is on record. Booking any of them runs the same availability check as the website, with the alert and a manager's reason for booking off hours, puts the address and vehicle the request brought on the customer's record, raises its review flags on the job, and assigns the default staff, or the crew checked when a quote request was priced. Only a manager prices or declines a request; a decline keeps its reason.
  - **Quote requests:** the vehicle, the service wanted with its condition level and add-ons, the description, the photos (tap one to see it full size), the address with its drive from the home base and its tax rate, the preferred days and the customer's number. A manager prices it in the Price section, with labeled fields: the service, the vehicle class, the price before tax, the **Time on site** (picked in 15-minute steps, shown as hours and minutes from 0:30 to 12:00) and the **Crew** (a checkbox for each active staff member, the default staff checked; the people checked are assigned when it's booked, and their number is the crew size) become a quote, the price of record ([decision 0059](../decisions/0059-quoted-price-is-price-of-record.md)), with the sales tax added. A request at condition Level 3 shows the Level 2 price for reference, and its quote keeps the level and add-ons ([decision 0103](../decisions/0103-service-levels-for-interior-work-and-level-3-by-quote.md)). Until Phase 4's texts, the owner sends the price to the customer by text or call; the text link fills in a message starting "Dixie Detail:". Then the request is booked from that quote into a time or declined. A booked request's job shows its description and photos, so whoever does the job sees them. Removing that job as created by mistake reopens the request with its quote; canceling the job closes the request ([decision 0102](../decisions/0102-removing-or-canceling-a-job-booked-from-a-request.md)). Photos are served only here, to a signed-in user, and are deleted 90 days after the request closes ([decision 0099](../decisions/0099-quote-requests-with-photos.md)).
  - **Offline requests:** sent while the server was offline, or Google's address suggestions were down, priced as they arrived; an address the customer typed is marked as not checked. Every one is kept, whatever it holds: a field that couldn't be read, such as a malformed phone number, is listed with the other things to check, and the screen shows **The request as it arrived** ([decision 0114](../decisions/0114-offline-requests-kept-whatever-they-hold.md)); "needs you" adds "something to check". A quote request sent the same way has no photos, shows the address as typed when it couldn't be checked, and notes any problem to check. The screen shows the price the customer saw beside today's, marked "Price differs" when they differ. Booking prices the job from the pricebook in effect on its day; **Honor the price the customer saw** makes the total before tax that price instead, with an adjustment line giving the reason.
  - **Booking requests:** a website booking the server took as a request instead of booking it ([decision 0113](../decisions/0113-one-answer-for-a-booking-that-becomes-a-request.md)): the customer already has a job that day (usually a second vehicle), or the booking was past a cap on unconfirmed bookings. The screen says which; the customer was never told. **Approve and book** books its quote, as the customer was shown it, at the time they picked or another. Before 2026-11-21 these were only the same-day case, called "Second vehicle the same day".
- **Vehicles no longer the customer's.** A vehicle's edit screen has **Still the customer's**; unticked, the vehicle stays on its jobs but is left out of quiet matching and the booking form, and the customer page marks it.

## The website's reviews

**Account → Reviews**, for managers only ([decision 0109](../decisions/0109-reviews-kept-in-the-control-app.md)), holds the customer reviews the website shows: the reviewer's name as shown (first name and last initial, as on Google), the text, its date, an optional star rating and an optional link to the Google review. Managers add, edit, move up or down, show or hide, and delete them; their change history is kept. Every saved change asks for a website rebuild through the deploy hook, as a pricebook publish does. The reviews are kept only in the database, never in the repository. The steps are in [../guides/managing-website-reviews.md](../guides/managing-website-reviews.md).

Website bookings record the customer's preferred payment method from the website's one list (Cash, Card, Venmo, PayPal, Cash App, Bitcoin); a Cash App choice is saved as Cash App Pay. Recording a payment keeps both Cash App routes ([decision 0110](../decisions/0110-one-website-list-of-payment-methods.md)).

## Arrival and departure taps

From Phase 4, a push at the scheduled arrival time will ask "Arrived?", and one tap records it, from the notification or the job screen; "Departed" works the same way, with a reminder at the expected end time. The control app compares actual time on site with the estimate, to show where service times need adjusting. The rules are in [scheduling-and-calendars.md](scheduling-and-calendars.md#arrival-and-departure-times-by-one-tap) and [decision 0062](../decisions/0062-arrival-and-departure-by-one-tap.md).
