# Privacy Policy

**Effective [the day the new site goes live]**

Dixie Detail LLC ("Dixie Detail", "we", "us") provides mobile vehicle detailing in southeast Tennessee. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

- **Contact details:** your name, mobile number and email address.
- **Service details:** the address where we perform the service, your vehicle's year, make and model, the services you book, and notes about the vehicle's condition.
- **Quote requests:** when you ask for a quote on our website, the description you write and any photos of your vehicle you choose to upload. When a photo arrives, we remove the hidden information stored in the file, including the location where it was taken.
- **Appointment and payment records:** dates, amounts, payment method and whether a payment was received. Card payments are handled by our payment processor, and we do not receive or store full card numbers.
- **Messages:** texts, calls, emails and form submissions you send us.
- **Website data:** pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location derived from your IP address, collected through cookies and similar tools.

## How we use it

- To schedule, perform and follow up on the services you request.
- To price a job you ask us to quote, using your description and photos.
- To check your service address, find the sales tax rate that applies to it, and estimate our drive time.
- To send appointment confirmations, reminders, arrival updates, payment links, receipts and a review request after your service.
- To answer your questions.
- To keep business, tax and accounting records.
- To understand how our website is used and to improve it.

## Text messages

We text you only if you have agreed to receive texts. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help. Our Text Message Terms have the full details.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third party, except with the service providers that deliver our messages on our behalf.

## Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

- **Service providers** that help us run the business, such as our booking and scheduling tools, phone and text messaging provider, payment processors, website host, email and calendar provider, accounting tools, map and address services, the bot check on our website forms, and off-site backup storage. They may use your information only to provide their service to us.
- **Address lookups:** to confirm a service address, find its sales tax rate and estimate drive time, we send the address to Google Maps and to the U.S. Census Bureau's address lookup service.
- **Staff** who perform your service, who see your name, address, vehicle and appointment details, and any description and photos from your quote request.
- **Authorities or other parties** when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our customers or the public.
- **A successor business**, if Dixie Detail is sold or merged, under the terms of this policy.

## Cookies and analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar tools to keep the site working. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the site will still work.

Our booking and quote forms use Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks information about your browser and device to tell real visitors from automated spam. When you type your address, Google Maps suggests matching addresses, so what you type is sent to Google.

## Photos

We may photograph a vehicle before and after service for our records. We may share photos of our work on our website and social media, without license plates or anything that identifies you. Tell us if you would prefer that we not share photos of your vehicle.

Photos you upload with a quote request are used only to price and prepare for your service. Only we and the staff who perform the service see them, and we never publish them or use them in marketing.

## How long we keep information

We keep customer and appointment records for as long as you are a customer and afterward for as long as tax, accounting and legal requirements call for. You can ask us to delete your information sooner, and we will do so unless we are required to keep it.

Photos you upload with a quote request are deleted 90 days after the request is declined, or the job it led to is done or canceled. Copies in our backups are removed as those backups expire, within about a year. The description you wrote stays with your records.

## Your choices

- Reply STOP to stop text messages at any time.
- Ask us what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it.
- Ask us not to share photos of your vehicle.
- Ask us to delete photos you uploaded with a quote request.

Send any request to support@dixiedetail.com or (423) 380-9120.

## Security

We limit access to customer information to the people and service providers who need it, and we protect it with reasonable safeguards. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure.

## Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and continued use of our services after a change means the updated policy applies.
